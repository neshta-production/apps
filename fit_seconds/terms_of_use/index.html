1. App Purpose

FitSeconds is a round timer for boxing, interval and circuit training. It counts rounds and rests, sounds and vibrates the signals, keeps a history of what you did, and can show and record heart rate from a Bluetooth monitor. It is a timer and a log — it is not a coach, a training programme, or a medical device.

Hard interval training carries real risk. Stop immediately and seek medical attention if you feel chest pain, faintness, severe breathlessness, or any symptom that concerns you — do not wait for a round to end because a timer is still running.
