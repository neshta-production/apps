FitSeconds

Support

Updated: October 6, 2026

FitSeconds is a round timer for boxing, interval and circuit training. If something is not working, or a signal did not sound when it should have, email safonchikigor@gmail.com and we will help.

1. What to Include

Most problems are quicker to fix with a few details:

  • Your device and operating-system version — for example "iPhone 13, iOS 18.2" or "Pixel 7, Android 15".
  • The App version, shown at the bottom of Settings.
  • The training you were running: how many rounds, the work and rest lengths, and whether it came from your saved list, a preset, or the quick timer.
  • For a monitor problem, the make and model of the strap or watch.
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead. A screenshot or a short screen recording usually settles it in one reply.

Please do not send health information unless it is genuinely needed to explain the problem.

2. A Signal Did Not Sound

Check that your device is not in silent mode or Do Not Disturb, that the bell volume in Settings is up, and that notifications are allowed for FitSeconds — the background timer uses them to sound signals when the App is not in front. On Android, battery optimisation can also stop a background run; allowing FitSeconds to run unrestricted fixes it.

3. A Heart-Rate Monitor Will Not Connect

Heart-rate monitoring is part of the subscription, and the App supports monitors that use the standard Bluetooth heart-rate profile.

  • ANT+-only straps cannot work with any phone app — including ours and the manufacturer's. Garmin HRM-Run, HRM-Tri and HRM 3.0/4.0 are in this group. The HRM-Dual, HRM-Fit, HRM-Pro and HRM-Pro Plus do work.
  • A Polar H10 allows one connection at a time unless two-device mode is enabled in Polar's own app. If it is paired to a bike computer or another app, release it there first.
  • Most watches cannot share heart rate with other apps on their own. For Apple Watch and Wear OS, install the FitSeconds companion on the watch and pick it in the monitor list.
  • Wet the strap's electrodes, and make sure the strap is not already connected to something else.

4. Trainings Are Saved Only on Your Device

FitSeconds has no account and no cloud sync, which means your trainings and history are not backed up by us. Uninstalling the App, or clearing its data, deletes them permanently and we cannot restore them. There is no way for us to recover a history that is gone, so export or note anything you cannot lose before reinstalling.

5. Subscriptions, Trials and Refunds

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple or Google, not by us.

  • To cancel, use the App Store subscription settings on iOS or Google Play subscriptions on Android. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.
  • Refunds are issued by Apple or Google under their own policies. We cannot issue or reverse one, but we can help you work out what went wrong.
  • If you have paid and the App still shows you as free, use Restore purchases in Settings. If that does not help, email us with the store receipt and we will look into it.

6. Ads and Privacy Choices

The free version shows ads; a subscription removes them. You can review or change your advertising consent at any time from the privacy options in Settings. What the App does and does not send off your device is set out in full in the Privacy Policy.

7. Health and Safety

FitSeconds is a timer, not a medical device, and the heart-rate figures it shows are training estimates rather than clinical measurements. Consult a physician before starting or changing a training programme, and stop training if you feel unwell. See section 2 of the Terms of Use.

8. Response Time

FitSeconds is made by a very small team, so replies are usually within a few days rather than the same hour. Bug reports with the details in section 1 get looked at first, because they are the ones that can actually be fixed.
