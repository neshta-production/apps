Calendout Support

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Need help with Calendout? Review the guidance below or contact us directly.

Email Support Privacy Policy Terms of Use

Contact Support

Email: imslowpoke@gmail.com

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Calendout version, selected game mode, and a short description of the issue. Screenshots are useful when they do not reveal private calendar information.

Never send your Apple Account password, verification codes, full payment card details, or calendar information unrelated to the issue.

Play Without Calendar Access

Select Developer Calendar from the main menu to play with built-in fictional events. This mode works without calendar permission and does not use your personal schedule.

Allow Calendar Access

  1. Open the iOS Settings app.
  2. Open Apps, then select Calendout.
  3. Open Calendars and allow full access.
  4. Return to Calendout and select iOS Calendar.

Calendout reads calendar data locally to build the game board. It does not upload calendar events.

Events Are Missing

Calendout displays events from the current Monday-through-Sunday week within its supported game window. Confirm that the event belongs to the current week, is not canceled, and is visible in the iOS Calendar app. All-day events and holidays appear at the top of the relevant day and are not breakable.

If events still do not appear, close and reopen Calendout after confirming permission. Calendar synchronization itself is managed by iOS and your calendar provider.

Achievements and Settings

Achievements, score milestones, onboarding completion, and preferences are stored locally on the device. Deleting the App may remove this local progress. The haptic setting is off by default and can be enabled from Calendout Settings.

Purchase, Redownload, or Refund Help

Calendout is a one-time App Store purchase with no subscription or in-app purchases. Apple handles payment, redownload, Family Sharing availability, purchase-history, and refund requests. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account used to purchase the App.

Privacy Requests

The App does not operate user accounts or store calendar data on our servers. You can revoke calendar access in iOS Settings and remove local app data by deleting the App. To request deletion of information you voluntarily sent in a support email, contact imslowpoke@gmail.com.
