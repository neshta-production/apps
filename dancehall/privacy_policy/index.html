Dancehall

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 7, 2026

Dancehall ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is a video-based dance learning app by Neshta Production. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information.

1. Privacy Summary

The App does not require an account and does not operate developer-controlled servers. We do not use advertising, third-party analytics, tracking technologies, or data brokers. The App does not automatically collect, upload, sell, rent, or share personal information.

2. Information Stored Locally

The App stores limited information only on your device using standard iOS local storage. This may include onboarding completion, subscription access status, completed lessons, video watch time, likes, current and best streaks, earned achievements, achievement dates, mute preferences, and other app settings.

This local information is used to provide progress tracking, rewards, personalization, and access to app features. It is not transmitted to us and is not available on our servers. Unless an Apple service independently restores an eligible purchase, deleting the App may permanently remove locally stored progress and preferences.

3. Video Lessons and Shorts

Video playback, likes, completion status, watch-time totals, and related progress are processed locally on your device. The App does not upload your viewing activity to us and does not create a user profile based on that activity.

4. Subscriptions and Payments

The App offers auto-renewable subscriptions that unlock access to all premium video lessons. Purchases, free trials, renewals, cancellations, refunds, and payment information are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit.

We do not receive or store your full payment card details or Apple Account password. The App may receive transaction and entitlement status from Apple only as needed to determine whether premium access is active. We do not combine that status with a developer-operated user account or advertising profile.

5. Device Permissions

The App does not require access to your contacts, precise location, photos, camera, microphone, health data, or advertising identifier for its core functionality. If a future version requests a new permission, the App will explain its purpose and this Privacy Policy will be updated where necessary.

6. Support Communications

If you choose Contact Us, the App opens an email composer addressed to our support email. The draft may include the device model, iOS version, and App version to help diagnose the issue. You can review the complete message before sending it.

If you send an email, we receive the email address, message, diagnostics, attachments, and any other information you voluntarily include. This information is used only to respond to your request, troubleshoot the App, maintain necessary support records, and comply with legal obligations. Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment card details, or unrelated sensitive information.

7. Tracking, Advertising, and Analytics

The App does not track you across apps or websites, access the advertising identifier, display third-party advertising, or include third-party analytics SDKs. Apple may independently process App Store downloads, purchases, crash reports, and diagnostics according to your Apple Account, device settings, and Apple’s own privacy policies.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Local progress remains on your device until you delete the App or clear its data. Because we do not receive that local information, we cannot view, recover, export, or delete it remotely.

Support emails are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to answer the request, keep appropriate support records, prevent abuse, or meet legal requirements. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing us from the same address used for the request.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is not specifically directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

10. Security

The App is designed to minimize data exposure by keeping progress and usage information on the device. No storage method is completely secure. Keep your device, Apple Account, and iOS version protected and up to date.

11. International Users

If you contact support, your message may be processed in countries where we or our email provider operate. Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding personal information included in support correspondence, including rights to access, correct, or delete it.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, Apple requirements, or applicable law changes. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. If a future version introduces accounts, servers, analytics, advertising, or additional data handling, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, visit Dancehall Support or email imslowpoke@gmail.com.
